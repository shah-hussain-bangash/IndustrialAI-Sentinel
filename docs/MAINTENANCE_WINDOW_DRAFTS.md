# Maintenance-window drafts — 27 November 2026

Workspace settings > Maintenance windows lets an engineer or administrator prepare an equipment-specific downtime window. Save a name, proposed start/end and reason, then revise it with an expected version. Every revision preserves its author, recorded time, interval and reason. A separate draft can be created without overwriting the selected one. Reloading the list does not overwrite the form; load the current draft to adopt another saved version.

**These are inactive planning records.** No suppression activation, cancellation, scheduler or rule-evaluation integration is included. Saving or revising a window never pauses a rule, suppresses a signal, changes an existing match/review, grants access, sends a notification or dispatches maintenance. A future increment must define and verify controlled activation and operational suppression separately. Overlapping or past draft intervals are permitted for planning/history and do not imply an effective window.

Times entered in the form are explicitly Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4), independently of browser timezone. The API accepts integral Unix seconds in the range 0 through 4102444800; end must follow start by at most seven days. Invalid calendar input and inconsistent ranges are blocked. Names are 1–120 characters; reasons 5–1000; whitespace-only/control-character text is rejected. A draft cannot move to another equipment item. There are at most 100 drafts per equipment item; revise an existing draft at the bound. Histories use version-keyed pages of 50.

API: `GET/POST /api/maintenance-windows/drafts`, `POST /api/maintenance-windows/drafts/{id}`, and `GET /api/maintenance-windows/drafts/{id}/history`. Listing requires `asset_id`; updates require `asset_id` and `expected_version`; older history uses `before_version`. The existing canonical equipment-site guard and engineer/admin role check apply. Writes require CSRF and idempotency keys, preserve immutable revisions, reject stale edits and record a scoped audit event. Account changes do not grant access to history outside current site permission. Only draft head/version data is mutable; no delete or activation endpoint exists in this increment.
